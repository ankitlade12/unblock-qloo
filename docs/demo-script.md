# Three-minute UNBLOCK presentation

Use a fresh workspace. Operations and participant decisions are fictional; Qloo discovery is live. Do not pre-record invented acceptance, bookings, or outcomes.

**0:00–0:25 — The person and the job.**
Open Overview, then the people chapter.
“An activity coordinator can find a film people like and still have an outing they cannot join. Jordan needs step-free travel and a companion seat. Morgan needs a hearing loop. UNBLOCK connects cultural interest to a plan that respects those requirements.”

**0:25–1:10 — Qloo's precise contribution.**
Discover → Alex → consent to the reviewed Inside Out profile → Load reviewed demo profile → Run Qloo discovery.
“Qloo connects a selected favorite to reviewed film identities. We keep the source evidence. A suggestion is not consent: I am explicitly recording this fictional participant's response.”
Show what actually returned. Previously Coco and The Grand Budapest Hotel were new options; do not promise those results on every request. If no new option returns, explain the result without substituting a fabricated response.

**1:10–1:50 — Follow the decision.**
Compare discovery → Freeze current choices → choose Qloo-backed suggestions → record an explicit response to a returned suggestion → Compare checked plans.
Scroll to **Follow each choice into the plan** and expand **Inspect checks and source evidence**.
“The same starting interests, budget, transport, and evaluation time are retained. This shows the favorite, provider evidence, explicit choice, practical checks, and whether the choice enters the checked plan. An accepted option can remain unselected. We do not manufacture an improvement.”
Download the comparison evidence if time permits. Never describe an individual compatibility check as a confirmed group booking.

**1:50–2:35 — Show the operational payoff.**
Return to the story. Generate a current candidate in The possibilities. Open The unexpected, cancel the resource used by the candidate, and check remaining options.
“Real plans change. Prior approvals stop being actionable; the organizer can inspect who changes, how cost changes, and what needs renewed review.”
Read the actual result. In the authored baseline demonstration, six assignments changed and cost rose from $224 to $246 while checked coverage remained 12. A different choice may produce a different result.

**2:35–3:00 — Evidence and next step.**
“Qloo supplies cultural connections; participants supply choices; the planner checks operational feasibility. This is a working, tested prototype with fictional operations. Our next test is a counterbalanced organizer study measuring planning time, missed requirements, and repair effort. We have not claimed real attendance or health improvements.”

For a full walkthrough beyond three minutes, show exact-version approval, participant commitments, provider confirmation, final export, and explicitly simulated post-event review. Team details and submission declarations remain the team's responsibility.
